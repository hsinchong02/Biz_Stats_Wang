How the density and statistics are calculated

Scaled bar height = 100 × bin count / (20,000 × bin width). The vertical axis is (Scaled) Percentage. Each bar’s area is its bin percentage, and all bar areas sum to 100%. The red polygon joins bin-midpoint heights and closes at zero one bin width beyond each outer midpoint; its total area is also 100%.

The dark-blue curve is the theoretical Uniform(0, 30) PDF using the specified bounds. Its scaled height is 100/30 = 3.333333 per minute from 0 through 30 and zero outside that range. The bounds are fixed at 0 and 30, rather than estimated from the sample minimum and maximum. Its theoretical mean is 15 minutes, variance is 75 minutes², and standard deviation is √75 ≈ 8.660254 minutes.

Empirical polygon interval probabilities integrate its straight segments exactly. The theoretical probability is the overlap of the selected interval with [0, 30], divided by 30. The empirical polygon smooths bin counts and may extend slightly beyond the actual bounds; this is an approximation. Equal-width histogram bins can also extend past 30 when the chosen width does not divide 30 exactly.

The statistic buttons calculate directly from the saved sample, separately from binned density estimates. Selecting one shows only its value; selecting another replaces it. All changes to bins, curves, and ranges reuse the same sample. The chart embeds exactly the same 20,000 values as the Excel data tab; seed 2150030.
