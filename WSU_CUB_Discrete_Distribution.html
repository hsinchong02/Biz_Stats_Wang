How the simulation works

Each simulated interval independently draws one of Group 13’s 50 recorded counts with equal chance, with replacement. Thus each value’s generating probability equals its observed Group 13 percentage. Larger samples tend to resemble that distribution; convergence need not be monotonic. Adjusting size uses the first n observations of the same generated million, so comparisons are nested. “Generate new sample” creates a fresh sequence.

One million observations are called a population here by teaching convention. A large sample does not become the actual population solely because of its size. The displayed probability distribution describes this finite simulated population, not all real WSU CUB arrivals.
